## The story

I'm Dinh, a software engineer. In 2026 I joined the [Happy Leadership Community (HLC)](https://hlcvn.org/) as a mentor. HLC pairs people with some life and work experience (mentors) with young people (mentees) to talk about how to live, learn and plan for the future.

I have the chance to mentor **Ngọc**, a grade-12 student from Quảng Ngãi, Vietnam. She is cheerful, confident and eager to learn, and she always comes with very concrete questions about her future. Before we met, she had already had several helpful sessions with other HLC mentors.

## What we do

- We meet online once a month, for about an hour, throughout her final year of high school.
- Each session has one theme: understanding yourself and choosing a path, studying effectively, preparing for the exams…
- Instead of slides, I turn each session into a web page with interactive exercises, so Ngọc can come back to it any time.

## You can use it too

The posts below talk directly to Ngọc, but the content is useful for any grade-12 student. If you are a student, a parent or another mentor, feel free to use and share them. Anything you type into the exercises is saved only on your own device.
