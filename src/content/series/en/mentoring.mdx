## The story

I'm a mentor with the [Happy Leadership Community (HLC)](https://hlcvn.org/). HLC pairs people with some life and work experience (mentors) with young people (mentees) to talk about how to live, learn and plan for the future.

Right now, I have the chance to mentor **Ngọc**, a grade-12 student from Quảng Ngãi, Vietnam.

## What we do

- We meet online three times, about once a month, for an hour each. After three sessions, HLC pairs the mentee with a new mentor, so she keeps hearing fresh perspectives.
- Each session has one theme: understanding yourself and choosing a path, studying effectively, preparing for the exams…

## You can use it too

Each post below is the material for one session, but I write them for any young person standing at a crossroads. If you are a student, a parent or another mentor, feel free to use and share them. Anything you type into the exercises is saved only on your own device.
